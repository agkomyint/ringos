RingOS (the kernel, the Ring language, compiler and VM) is **source-available**, not open source.
It is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE): you may read, build, modify
and share it for personal use, learning, research, hobby projects, schools and charities.
**Commercial use needs a separate licence.**

## Who needs what
| You are | You get |
|---|---|
| Hobbyist, student, researcher | Free: use the code under `LICENSE` |
| Bought RingOS arcade hardware from us | A licence to run the RingOS that ships on that device. No redistribution |
| Bought RingOS itself | A licence for the terms on the purchase |
| A company that wants RingOS on its own hardware, or in a product | A commercial licence from us: open an issue or contact the owner |

## Your games are yours
Games you write in Ring belong to you. You may sell them, give them away, and license them however
you like. The licence covers RingOS and the Ring toolchain, not the programs you write with it.

Contributions are welcome. By sending a pull request you agree that the owner may license your
contribution under both `LICENSE` and the commercial licences above.
